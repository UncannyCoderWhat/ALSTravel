Places we offer

3 destinations

El Nido, Palawan

Dramatic limestone cliffs, white sand beaches and turquoise waters. Discover hidden lagoons, secret coves and vibrant coral reefs on an island hopping escape.

Boracay

Powdery white sand beaches, azure waters and vibrant nightlife. A compact tropical escape that brings together coastal beauty and lively island experiences.

Siargao Island

The Philippines' surfing capital, with palm forests, tidal rock pools and world-class surf breaks. Experience laid back island culture and rich natural ecosystems.

Vigan, Ilocos Sur

Walk along historic Calle Crisologo, admiring century old Spanish colonial architecture, cobblestone avenues, and traditional horse drawn kalesas.

Banaue Rice Terraces

Hand carved into the mountains by indigenous ancestors thousands of years ago, these awe inspiring terraces showcase scenic mountain trekking and living heritage.

Mayon Volcano, Albay

Embark on scenic ATV road adventures through volcanic trails and black lava walls while admiring the world famous symmetrical cone of Mount Mayon.

Coron, Palawan

Sail across mirror like freshwater lakes, snorkel through sunken World War shipwrecks, and cruise through majestic limestone sea karsts and hidden coves.

Apo Reef, Mindoro

The second largest contiguous coral reef system in the world, offering world class marine diving, sea turtle encounters, and crystal clear ocean waters.

Hundred Islands

Charter an island hopping boat across one hundred mushroom shaped emerald isles, exploring sea caves, zip lines, and secluded sandbars.
